Full console (xterm). Prefer Accessible terminal with a screen reader for form-style work. Press Alt+F2 for a read-only text snapshot of the buffer.

Settings Accessible terminal

Accessible buffer

Read-only snapshot of the terminal. Use browse mode or arrow keys to read. Previous/Next line buttons, or Ctrl+Up/Down, or Alt+P/N move by line and announce. Escape or Close returns to the console.
